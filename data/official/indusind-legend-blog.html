Top benefits of having IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card

Top benefits of having IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card

Updated on Wednesday, October 21st, 2026 | By IndusInd Bank

Credit cards have evolved into indispensable products in today’s financial landscape. They offer not only purchasing power but also a host of additional benefits, such as enhanced security features and rewarding perks. This improves the overall financial experience.

Among the range of credit cards available, the IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card stands out as a prime choice. It offers lifetime free benefits with no joining or annual fees and a host of exclusive features that elevate your experience. Whether you are a frequent traveller, a foodie, a movie buff, or a shopaholic, you can apply for this credit card online and get rewards and privileges on travel, dining and lifestyle.

Key benefits of IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card

IndusInd Bank offers benefits in multiple categories with the Legend Credit Card. Here is a breakdown of some of its key features and advantages:

Reward points

With the IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card, you have the opportunity to earn 1 reward point for every ₹100 spent on weekdays and 2 reward points for every ₹100 spent on weekends.

Each reward point is valued at ₹0.75 in cash. For instance, if you spend ₹90,000 on a weekend, you redeemable for ₹1,350 in cash. (Note: Prices might change and hence it’s vital to check the price before redemption)

Unlock generous bonus points

The IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card offers an exclusive bonus point benefit on spending ₹6 lakhs or more within 1 year from the date of issue. You have the opportunity to earn 4,000 bonus reward points. These points can be quite valuable, offering extra opportunities for redemption, including conversion to cash credit or utilisation in specific merchant categories. This feature significantly enhances the card’s value.

Travel and other benefits

The IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card is designed to elevate your travel experiences to unprecedented heights.

With access to over 600 VIP airport lounges worldwide through the Priority Pass programme, you can relax and rejuvenate during layovers or flight delays. What’s more, the bank’s unique ‘Travel Plus’ programme provides you with an array of amenities to make your wait for connecting flights an absolute pleasure.

Additionally, the IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card offers a 1.8% discounted foreign currency mark-up fee on international transactions, making your global adventures not only luxurious but also cost-effective.

Premium concierge services

The IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card offers an exceptional concierge service, providing you with a dedicated team of highly efficient professionals accessible from anywhere in the world. This complimentary service goes above and beyond to assist with various aspects of travel and lifestyle.

Whether you need information, recommendations, or assistance for your upcoming trip, the concierge is there to help. They offer insights on travel destinations, weather, landmarks, and transportation options. They can seamlessly handle hotel reservations at top-notch establishments globally, as well as make flight arrangements to ensure you reach your desired destination hassle-free.

Moreover, the concierge extends its support to securing tickets for sports or recreational events, booking seats for workshops or seminars, and even arranging special gifts or flower deliveries worldwide.

Contactless payments

The IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card is designed for unparalleled convenience, featuring chip-based contactless technology that ensures swift, secure, and hassle-free transactions in your everyday life. You can apply for this instant credit card online and effortlessly make payments at merchant locations that support contactless payments, enabling faster and more convenient day-to-day purchases.

It opens up a world of possibilities with access to over 10,00,000 merchant outlets within India and a staggering 30 million outlets globally that accept card payments. Beyond in-person shopping, you can also use this credit card for various online transactions, including booking movie tickets and flight tickets, and handling utility bill payments.

This card truly empowers you to embrace the digital age of banking and enjoy the freedom and flexibility it offers for your financial needs.

Conclusion

Experience the epitome of financial luxury as you apply for the prestigious IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card and unlock exclusive access to a world of premium benefits. From unparalleled reward points and customized travel perks to concierge services that redefine convenience, this card offers a wealth of advantages tailored to your lifestyle. It ensures you enjoy the utmost financial sophistication and rewards. Apply now to embark on a truly exceptional financial experience.

Seize this opportunity to elevate your financial journey. Apply for an IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card online today!
